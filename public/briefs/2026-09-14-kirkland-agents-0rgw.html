The interview

Five questions

Same five, same order, every candidate. Send the plans, site, and price target three days ahead so question 3 is a real test.

1. Walk us through your last three sales inside Kirkland: address, list price, sold price, days on market. And give us one past seller, ideally a builder, we can call this week.

Listen for: three addresses from memory, honest numbers, and a phone number offered on the spot.

2. Have you sold a DADU, or a home with a DADU, in Kirkland? Which one, how did you price the unit, and how did the appraiser and the buyer treat it?

Listen for: a specific address, a number on the unit, and what the appraiser actually did. A generic answer means they have not.

3. Price this project today. Show us the comps you would use and the number you would list at.

Listen for: arrived with comps pulled, a range with reasoning, a list price. "I'd want to see it finished" is a fail.

4. In Kirkland, which finishes and features actually move the sale price, and which ones do buyers refuse to pay for?

Listen for: specifics backed by their own sales, and at least one thing they would tell Shiraz to cut. Everything "adds value" means they do not know.

5. Commission, term length, cancellation, who on your team actually shows the house and answers the phone, and what would you do if the listing sits 30 days with no offers?

Listen for: clear numbers, a term under six months, a real exit, the name of who will be at the door, and a day-30 plan where the price cut is one tool among several.
