Taking on more of the deal

Aldo, this is every job in a deal, from the first call to the closing, and who owns each one. Green is yours. Gold is Randy's. The shaded green boxes marked NEW or IMPROVE are the jobs that are either new to you or need work. Those are the ones we are building toward.

1

Find the seller

Cold call from the dialer, or agent outreachAldoBoth are sources of leads. Confirm the owner and the address. If they would consider selling, mark it as an opportunity.
OnboardingRandyThe lead is created in the app with the call recording attached and goes on the board as Onboarding. The app pulls the county record and property details at this point, usually on its own.
2

Learn the property

Gather all the infoAldoWork the lead until you have all of it:
  • Occupancy
  • Condition, 1 to 10
  • Timeline
  • Their asking price
  • Photos
Photos are the one item that can come back as "not possible." Everything else has no excuse.
Propose a rangeAldoLearn how to run comps, how to value a property, and how to determine an offer range. Learn it on your own, and ask Randy questions anytime.
No green check until both are doneA lead is not ready for Randy until the info bar is full and a range is proposed. If either is missing, the lead stays with you.
Can't reach them? The hourglass rule7 calls and 3 texts with no contact. Then put ⏳ on the right side of the lead's line and move on. Randy decides whether it gets parked or keeps going.
3

Present the offer

Confirm the priceRandyRandy reviews the range and confirms the price we present.
Present the offerAldoRandy will instruct how to present it, by text or by call. Follow that.
4

Get it under contract

Write and send the contractRandyRandy puts the PSA together, sends it to the seller, and updates the lead.
Confirm they got it and answer questionsAldoMake sure they have it and answer what you can. Any change they ask for goes to Randy.
5

Market it

Make the marketing pageRandyOnce the contract is signed, a marketing page for investors gets created.
Send it to matching investorsAldoThe app queues the text and email to every investor who buys in that area. Review the list, send. Randy will teach you how.
Call every investor it went toAldoSame day as the send. Interested, passed, or silent. Log it on the investor record. Randy does not step in until a buyer needs more information or wants to proceed. Those get a green check. A pass gets an X.
Send the buyer the assignment contractRandyOnce a buyer is proceeding.
6

Close it

Open escrowRandySigned assignment and contract to the title company. Earnest money deposited.
Oversee the closingRandyFacilitate between the buyer, the seller, and the title company until it closes. Fee paid at closing.
