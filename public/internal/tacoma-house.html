The rules

The house is in Parkland, unincorporated Pierce County, so Tacoma’s city code does not apply. Washington state law does.

Washington
  • Deposit: a signed move-in condition checklist, the deposit held in a trust account with the bank named in writing, an itemized return within 30 days of move-out. The tenant can pay it in installments if they ask.
  • Screening: disclose the criteria, the screening service and the fee before running it, and give a written reason if someone is declined. Lawful income counts, vouchers included. Families with children and assistance animals cannot be screened out.
  • Rent increases: none in the first 12 months, then capped at 7% plus inflation, 10% at most, with 90 days’ notice (state law since 2025).
  • Entry: two days’ written notice to enter an occupied unit.
  • Disclosures in the lease: lead paint (pre-1978 house), the state mold information, fire safety.
  • Working smoke and CO alarms, and a habitable house, are the landlord’s duty.
