What gets built
The databaseThe official record of everything The DADU Guy buys: every item, vendor, price, and lead time, kept current. The knowledge that runs the whole system.
Automated order sheetsThe moment a client finishes selections, the full order sheet exists: every item, quantity, vendor, price, and order-by date. You approve; it moves.
Your approval pageThe one page you touch. Confirm quantities, set your price with your true cost on screen, approve each order batch. Minutes, not afternoons.

The Projects PageOpen any project: selections, renders, CO status, orders, deliveries. The “what did this client pick?” call stops existing.
Weekly automated database upkeepEvery week, an AI sweeps the catalogue against the vendors and flags what moved. What changed lands in a weekly email to you and Randy, so reviews are seen, not just run. Your quotes ride current numbers.
Order execution paid per buildThe one deliberately manual step. Randy places every order from the staged batches, so a person always stands between the system and the money. Automation’s speed, with human judgment on every purchase. Billed once per project, as builds come.
The main components

The database

The official record of everything The DADU Guy buys, in one place, kept current. Every option a client can pick has a real product sitting behind it, and that product is the one we order. When something is discontinued, replaced or goes up in price, it is corrected once, here, and it stays right for every build after.

Data points collected on every item:

The itemthe exact one we buy, with a link straight to it
The tierbasic, enhanced or premium
The sourcewhere we buy it, plus a backup when stock runs out
The pricecurrent cost, reviewed weekly
The lead timeorder to delivery, per vendor
How it is countedby the piece, by the square foot, by the linear foot
The photothe same image the client picked from

The DADU Guy app

Every project gets a page. Open it and everything is there: selections, renders, the change order and its status, the order sheet, and what has been ordered. Everything you do happens here too: confirming quantities, setting the price, approving batches, all from the project page. And the database is always available for review.

How it works
Randy Creates the projectThe moment the designs are uploaded, the project exists in the app, and quantities are computed straight from the drawings, with the math shown.
Client Finishes selectionsExactly as they do today. Nothing changes on their side.
Shiraz Prices the change orderYou open the project page, confirm the quantities, review and pre-approve the order sheet, and set the change order price, with the true cost of the selections already on screen. One sitting, a few minutes.
System The client’s package is generatedThey receive a link by email: their selections, a few 3D renders of what they chose, and the price, with the change order built in. They click Approve, and the completed change order lands in both your inboxes. Approving carries the same legal weight as signing it.
Client Approves, or doesn’tIf they approve, it is done. If they hold off, they get a call, Shiraz straightens out whatever stopped them, and the package is resent. Money is agreed before any of it is spent.
Shiraz Approves each order batchThe order sheet groups itself by when things must be ordered. One look per batch. Nothing is bought until he says so.
Randy Runs the order runEach vendor ordered against its own lead time, every order tracked from placed to delivered to verified on site, so materials land when the trades need them instead of weeks early or late.
